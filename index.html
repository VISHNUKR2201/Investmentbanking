Our Growth Journey

2016

Founding Stackly

Stackly was founded in New York with a simple mission: democratize high-end financial advice for mid-market businesses. We launched with a team of 5 seasoned wall street experts.

  • Established NYC headquarters
  • Acquired first 50 corporate accounts
  • Launched proprietary portfolio analysis tool
2018

Going Nationwide

After successfully securing $10M in Series A funding, we expanded operations across 12 states, establishing regional offices in Chicago, Austin, and San Francisco.

  • AUM reached $250 Million
  • Formed tax-efficiency consultancy division
  • Featured in Forbes Capital Highlights
2021

AI Portfolio Advisory

Integrating advanced machine learning models into our advisory stream allowed real-time portfolio rebalancing and tax-loss harvesting for all client categories.

  • Launched AI portfolio optimizer
  • Surpassed $1 Billion assets under advisement
  • Voted Best Finance Advisor by FinTech Review
2024

Global Reach

Stackly expanded into European and Asian markets. We now operate across 25 offices worldwide, serving clients in over 40 countries with specialized localized tax strategies.

  • AUM reached $3.5 Billion
  • Established London & Singapore hubs
  • Launched Legacy Trust & Philanthropy solutions
