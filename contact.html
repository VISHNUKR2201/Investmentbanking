Appointment & Contact FAQs

How quickly can I schedule an in-person advisory meeting?

Initial virtual intake consultations can be scheduled within 24 to 48 hours. In-person meetings at our Salem, Coimbatore, or Bangalore offices require 3 business days advance confirmation.

What documents should I bring to an initial audit session?

We recommend preparing your recent corporate tax filings, existing entity structures, and asset allocation summaries. All documents submitted are protected by our strict Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).

Are consultation requests completely confidential?

Yes. All communications submitted through our contact form or hotline are governed by legal privilege and high-grade cryptographic encryption in full accordance with OECD secrecy guidelines.

Can I request consultation in an international timezone?

Our global advisory desk accommodates clients in US, European, and Asian time zones with round-the-clock video conference availability.
