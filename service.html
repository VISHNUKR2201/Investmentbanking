Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a corporate structure overhaul take?

Typically, a diagnostic discover assessment is completed in 10-14 business days. The actual setup and implementation of cross-border holding structures or offshore corporate vehicles takes 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the speed of local registries.

Do you coordinate with my local tax legal counsel?

Yes, we coordinate directly with your existing accountants, local CPAs, and family legal representatives to ensure that changes seamlessly integrate with your national domestic filings and local reporting rules.

Are offshore structures fully compliant with national tax laws?

Absolutely. We only implement structures that operate in full transparency under statutory law. All foreign accounts and structures are structured to follow automatic disclosures, FATCA, and local CRS guidelines without exception.

What is the minimum asset value for advisory setup?

Our strategic structuring and advisory services are most effective for private assets or corporate values exceeding $500,000. However, we also provide flat-rate consultation reviews for earlier-stage high-growth startups.
